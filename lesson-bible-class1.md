# 📘 BuildBots AI Lesson Bible — Class 1: The Secret Inventor's Lab & Flashlight Discovery

---

## 1. Lesson Overview

- **Lesson Number**: Lesson 1 (Month 1, Class 1)
- **Lesson Name**: The Secret Inventor's Lab & Flashlight Discovery
- **Month**: Month 1 (Virtual Robotics Lab & Circuit Basics)
- **Duration**: 30–45 Minutes
- **Difficulty**: Beginner (Level 1)
- **Age Group**: Age 7–12 (Optimized for 7-year-olds)
- **Learning Objective**: Children discover that a robot is a machine that does jobs, and that electricity flows in a complete circuit loop from a battery through wires to power an LED light.
- **Real-World Application**: Flashlights, bedroom light switches, toy cars, emergency lanterns, and night lamps.
- **Prerequisite Knowledge**: None (First introductory class).
- **Expected Outcome**: Child identifies 5 core electronics components (Battery, LED, Switch, Breadboard, Wire), understands basic energy flow, and interacts with Tinkercad/BuildBots UI.
- **Today's Badge**: 🏅 Junior Robot Explorer & 🔋 Battery Hunter
- **Today's XP**: +150 XP
- **Power Crystal Earned**: 💎 Yellow Energy Crystal (Energy Core)
- **Today's Hardware**: 9V Battery, Red LED, SPST Slide Switch, Breadboard, Jumper Wires
- **Today's Software**: BuildBots Interactive UI & Tinkercad Circuits Environment
- **Today's Build**: Simple Battery-to-LED Light Circuit (The Flashlight Project)
- **Safety Notes**: Never touch bare wires directly to wall outlets; battery energy stays in low-voltage lab circuits.
- **Parent Takeaway**: "Today your child became a certified Junior Robot Explorer! They learned how batteries feed energy to lights and identified 5 key components in electronics."

## 2. Story Bible

- **Story Title**: *Bolt and the Mysterious Sleeping Lab*
- **Opening Hook**: A glowing purple mission envelope sealed with a gold wax gear seal arrives directly from the Robot Factory.
- **Main Problem**: Bolt the Robot is frozen in the dark laboratory with zero power. His eyes are dark and he cannot move without finding his Energy Lunchbox (Battery) and Flashlight Eye (LED).
- **Why Bolt Needs Help**: Bolt doesn't know how to connect wires to make electricity flow to his flashlight eye.
- **Today's Mystery**: *What makes a sleeping robot wake up and do jobs?*
- **Mission Goal**: Open the Vault Door, find 5 hidden circuit treasures, and wire Bolt's flashlight eye to turn ON!
- **Characters**:
  - **Bolt**: Cute, curious, energetic cyan robot mascot (*emotions: excited, sleepy, thinking, heroic*).
  - **The Student (Chief Inventor)**: Gives commands, makes choices, and solves puzzles.
- **Emotions**: Mystery → Wonder → High Energy → Curiosity → Triumph → Cliffhanger Suspense.
- **Beginning**: A secret envelope invites the child to enter the Secret Inventor's Lab.
- **Middle**: Bolt is asleep! The student plays mini-games to collect the 5 circuit components (Battery lunchbox, LED flashlight eye, Switch clicker, Wire highways, Breadboard LEGO base).
- **Ending**: The student completes the circuit, Bolt's flashlight eye glows bright red, and Bolt dances with delight!
- **Victory**: Unlocking the 5-Star Robotics Hero Badge & Junior Explorer Certificate.
- **Cliffhanger**: As Bolt celebrates, the room dims, the light flickers, and a mysterious locked switch appears on the far wall with a strange glowing purple keyhole...

## 4. Scene Details

### Scene 1: Mission Envelope Arrival
- **Scene Number**: 1
- **Scene Name**: Mission Envelope Arrival
- **Learning Goal**: Establish curiosity and mission identity.
- **Story Goal**: Hook attention; invite child to become a Robot Inventor.
- **Visual Goal**: Deep space blueprint grid background with a hovering purple envelope sealed with gold wax.
- **Scene Description**: Holographic purple envelope pulses center-stage. Sparkles float around it.
- **Bolt Dialogue**: *"A secret message for YOU! Tap it!"*
- **Teacher Dialogue**: *"Today something special happened... a secret envelope arrived from the Robot Factory!"*
- **Student Interaction**: Tap the wax stamp to open the scroll.
- **Animation Notes**: Envelope hovers up/down; golden light rays burst when clicked.
- **Transition**: Zoom into scroll unrolling (0.5s pop).
- **Estimated Time**: 1 min

### Scene 2: Inventor's Code & HUD Tutorial
- **Scene Number**: 2
- **Scene Name**: Inventor's Code & HUD Tutorial
- **Learning Goal**: Introduce 5-star reward HUD & emotional safety (mistakes are cool).
- **Story Goal**: Lock in the child's superpower choice.
- **Visual Goal**: 4 glassmorphism cards (Yellow, Pink, Cyan, Lime) popping out with 3D icons.
- **Scene Description**: Cards pop up introducing rules: 1) Spot clues, 2) Mistakes are cool, 3) Super curiosity, 4) Silly guesses.
- **Bolt Dialogue**: *"Which rule is your favorite?"*
- **Teacher Dialogue**: *"Every mission has rules — but these are FUN rules! Which one is your superpower?"*
- **Student Interaction**: Pick one card to lock in Star #1.
- **Animation Notes**: Cards stagger-pop (0.2s delay); Star #1 flies into HUD meter with chime sound.
- **Transition**: Slide left transition.
- **Estimated Time**: 2 min

### Scene 3: Meet Sleepy Bolt
- **Scene Number**: 3
- **Scene Name**: Meet Sleepy Bolt
- **Learning Goal**: Understand that machines require energy to function.
- **Story Goal**: Introduce Bolt's problem (empty battery).
- **Visual Goal**: Dim moonlight room with sleeping Bolt sitting on the floor; Zzz bubbles floating.
- **Scene Description**: Bolt's screen-eyes are dark. 3 choices appear: Apple, Battery, Pillow.
- **Bolt Dialogue**: *"I need... power... Zzz..."*
- **Teacher Dialogue**: *"Look at Bolt! He's completely sleeping. How can we give him energy?"*
- **Student Interaction**: Click the ⚡ Battery card to charge Bolt.
- **Animation Notes**: Zzz bubbles float; selecting Battery shoots a lightning beam that lights Bolt's eyes yellow.
- **Transition**: Scale-up glow pulse.
- **Estimated Time**: 2 min

## 7. Interactive Activities

### Activity: Spot the Robot Lunchbox (Battery Match)
- **Activity Name**: Spot the Lunchbox
- **Goal**: Identify that batteries store electrical energy for circuit components.
- **Instructions**: Tap the item that feeds power to the robot!
- **Expected Answer**: `🔋 9V Battery` card.
- **Wrong Feedback**: *"❌ Light bulbs eat power, they don't store it! Try again!"*
- **Correct Feedback**: *"✅ BINGO! The battery is the robot's lunchbox!"*
- **Difficulty**: Beginner (Level 1)
- **Reward**: ⭐ +25 XP + Star #4 Unlocked
- **Replay**: Yes, instant retry on wrong choice
- **Accessibility**: ARIA labels on all option buttons, high contrast border highlights

## 8. Teacher Script

- **Ask**: *"Who thinks this teddy bear can walk to the kitchen on its own?"*
- **Pause**: 10 seconds (*Let children think and giggle*).
- **Explain**: *"Right! Teddy stays frozen. But a ROBOT has a battery inside that gives it power to DO JOBS!"*
- **Demonstrate**: Hold up a real 9V battery and Red LED light to the camera.
- **Question**: *"If the battery is the robot's lunchbox... what color do you think Bolt's flashlight eye will glow when we connect it?"*
- **Celebrate**: *"BOOM! High five! You just wired your first robot circuit!"*
